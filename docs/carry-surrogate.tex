{\small 8-layer TinyStories models of the K+J notes, \code{code\_residual}, abs-TopK over \(N=1536\), \(T=2\), MD optimizer, one seed.

\section{The problem}

With the code carried between blocks hard Top-\(K'\) gains \(0.06\)--\(0.075\) nats at every \(K'\) and RBLapSum
(\(K\) active, pool \(K{+}J\)) gains \(-0.03\) to \(+0.015\): at a matched active count it is worth \(0.047\) at
\(K=32\), loses \(0.02\)--\(0.03\) at \(K=128,256\), no cell reaches hard Top-\((K{+}J)\), and its backward is the
unstable part of the grid (\code{docs/kj-vs-hard-topk-code-residual.tex}); the first-order scope removes the
instability at the price of a second backward pass and is \emph{worse} than it at every \(K=32\) cell.
This note locates the conflict and removes it at no cost.

\section{Where the probabilities act}

Gate \(\ell\) reads \(u_\ell=c_\ell+v_\ell\) (carried code plus the block's encoded contribution) and outputs
\(c_{\ell+1}=M_\ell u_\ell\), \(M_\ell\) the Top-\(K\) mask.  Its output has two consumers, the carry
\(u_{\ell+1}=c_{\ell+1}+v_{\ell+1}\) (a skip path) and the read \(x_{\ell+1}=\mathrm N(D_\ell c_{\ell+1})\) (a branch):
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial u_\ell}=M_\ell\bigl(g^{\rm c}_{\ell+1}+r_{\ell+1}\bigr),\qquad
  g^{\rm c}_{\ell+1}=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial u_{\ell+1}},\quad
  r_{\ell+1}=D_\ell^{\!\top}\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(D_\ell c_{\ell+1})}
  \label{eq:hard}
\end{equation}
(\(r\) is the unembedding's gradient at the last gate).  In the stream-carried stack the gate has only the second
consumer: everything block \(\ell{+}1\) does, its own gate included, goes through \(D_\ell\).  RBLapSum relaxes the gate
as a whole, \(M_\ell\to M_\ell+S_\ell\) (kernel \(\kappa_i=e^{-|s_i-b|/T}/2T\); \(S_{ii}=|u_i|\kappa_i\approx b/2T\) at
the boundary).  In the stream stack \(S_\ell\) acts on \(r_{\ell+1}\) alone and
reaches gate \(\ell{-}1\) through \(E_\ell\), the block and \(D_{\ell-1}\), which mix the coordinates.  In the code stack
it also acts on \(g^{\rm c}_{\ell+1}\), which already contains \(S_{\ell+1}g^{\rm c}_{\ell+2}\): along a coordinate near
the boundary the backward multiplies \(1+|u|\kappa\) per gate.  The carry is a residual connection in code space,
trainable because its backward is the identity; a relaxation on the skip path compounds.  The conflict is \emph{where}
the probabilities act, not the kernel.

\section{The carry-aware surrogate}

Keep the skip path hard, relax the branch: the gate's output is split into the copy the next gate carries and the
copy the decoder reads (forward and inference unchanged), and
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial u_\ell}=M_\ell\bigl(g^{\rm c}_{\ell+1}+r_{\ell+1}\bigr)+S_\ell^{\!\top}\hat g_{\ell+1},
  \qquad
  \hat g_{\ell+1,i}=r_{\ell+1,i}+\begin{cases}0 & \text{local}\\ H_{\ell+1,i} & \text{hard}\\ \rho R_{\ell+1,i} & \text{persistent,}\end{cases}
  \label{eq:carry}
\end{equation}
with \(H_\ell=M_\ell(r_{\ell+1}+H_{\ell+1})\) the downstream read gradients a coordinate reaches along the hard carry,
\(R_\ell=r_{\ell+1}+\rho R_{\ell+1}\) every downstream read with a survival factor \(\rho\) per gate, \(H=R=0\) above
the last gate.  The support term is driven by read gradients only, so no kernel sees another gate's support term
through the carry: the products are gone, the terms of the gates above still reach gate \(\ell\) added through
\(M_\ell\), carry and update receive one gradient (R1 of \code{docs/rblapsum-code-residual.tex}), and the cost is two
per-coordinate sums along the backward, no second pass.  The drivers are the first-order loss change of one flip at
gate \(\ell\) (\(c_{\ell+1,i}\) changes by \(\pm u_{\ell,i}\); block \(\ell{+}1\) reads it, later blocks while the
coordinate stays in the code) under three models of what follows.  \emph{Local}: the next read only -- the stream
stack's logic, exactly the gradient of a model whose decoder reads a relaxed gate while the carry is hard.
\emph{Hard}: the actual downstream masks (an evicted feature's remaining run; for an inactive candidate almost always
nothing).  \emph{Persistent}: an entering coordinate survives each further gate with probability \(\rho\).
\emph{Mixed}: hard for the active members, persistent for the inactive ones.  (First order takes every gate hard,
hence its second pass; here only the carry.)

\section{Measurements}

\textbf{Setup.}  The code-carried \(T=2\) arm of the K+J grid (no output norm, batch \(96\times512\), the 20k-step
cosine schedule, seed 1337).  Baselines are the grid's runs read at matching steps; each new scope trains the grid's config with one field changed, stopped at step 3000
with the schedules untouched, two cells continued to 10k.  At step 3000 the grid's surrogate margins are largest and already of their final sign
(\code{pool}: \(-0.061\to-0.042\) from 3k to 20k at \(K=32\), \(+0.048\to+0.034\) at \(K=256\)).

\textbf{At initialization} (Fig.~\ref{fig:mech}a,b) the surrogate's share of the gradient energy at the gates,
\(\sigma=\lVert S^\top\hat g\rVert^2/\lVert m\odot g\rVert^2\), is \(0.70\) for \code{pool}, \(0.74\) for mixed,
\(0.31\) for local and \(0.24\) for first order at \(K=32\) (\(0.30\), \(0.20\), \(0.09\), \(0.09\) at \(K=256\)): the
mixed scope injects as much support signal as the unmodified backward, as a sum instead of a product.

\textbf{Runs of the carried code} (Fig.~\ref{fig:mech}c,d; 16 validation sequences, hard forward).  The carry is
persistent for a subset of the code, not for the code: in the trained hard Top-32 model every gate evicts
\(30\)--\(54\%\) of the support it receives, an entered feature is still there one gate later with probability
\(0.51\) and two later \(0.34\), \(12\%\) of the final code was carried from gate 0 and \(54\%\) entered at the last
gate (hard Top-256: \(0.57\), \(0.41\), \(17\%\), \(34\%\)); an entry at gate 0 is read by \(2.7\)--\(3.1\) blocks on
average, against 8 under the persistent model and 1 under the local one.  The \code{pool} surrogate trains the
model \emph{out of} the carry -- at \((32,224)\) turnover \(63\%\) per gate at step 4000 and \(70\%\) at 20k, survival
\(0.21\to0.16\), \(77\%\) of the final code entering at the last gate: the stream stack's re-selection at every block,
in a model that has a carry.  Every carry-scope model keeps
the hard model's code: survival \(0.52\)--\(0.61\), turnover \(30\)--\(40\%\) at step 3000, and at 10k
\(0.47\)/\(45\%\) and \(0.59\)/\(33\%\) for the mixed \((32,224)\) and \((256,256)\) models (hard at 20k: \(0.51\)/\(39\%\),
\(0.57\)/\(32\%\)).

\textbf{Training} (Fig.~\ref{fig:training}).  At \((32,224)\) the local scope ends step 3000
at \(-0.058\) below hard Top-32, where \code{pool} is at \(-0.061\) and first order at \(-0.033\); it leads \code{pool}
by \(0.02\) at step 1000 and the two coincide from 2000.  The mixed scope ends at \(-0.118\), twice the margin of the
unmodified surrogate and the margin of hard Top-128 over hard Top-32 at that step (\(-0.124\)): 32 active features at
the loss of the hard model that activates 128 (\(1.782\) against \(1.776\)).  Continued to 10k its margin decays as
the grid's do, \(-0.109\) at 5k and \(-0.100\) at 10k, against hard Top-128 at \(-0.121\), \(-0.117\), hard Top-64 at
\(-0.064\), \(-0.063\) and \code{pool} at \(-0.054\), \(-0.045\).  At \((256,256)\), the pathological cell (\code{pool} \(+0.047\) at 3000, its code turning
over \(46\%\) per gate against the hard model's \(28\%\)), the local scope closes the gap to \(+0.009\) and the mixed
scope is below hard Top-256 from step 2000, \(-0.0045\) at 3000 (\(1.736\) against \(1.741\)): a surrogate ahead of
the hard model with the same active count, which no code-carried cell of the grid achieved (hard Top-512: \(-0.037\);
the hard baselines also carry a post-norm, worth about \(0.007\) to a hard model in the stream grid).  Continued to 10k the lead widens, \(-0.008\) at 5k and \(-0.014\) at 10k, with \code{pool} at \(+0.037\),
\(+0.031\) and hard Top-512 at \(-0.034\), \(-0.029\).  On the diagonal \(J=K\), the stream grid's winning configuration, the mixed scope is below hard Top-\(K\)
from step 500 on at \((128,128)\) and ends at \(-0.006\) (\code{pool} \(+0.025\); hard Top-256 \(-0.035\)), and
\PH{} at \((64,64)\) (\code{pool} \(-0.005\); hard Top-128 \(-0.059\)); with the measured
survival \(\rho=0.5\) in the persistent chain \PH{}.  No carry-scope run's gradient norm exceeded 1 (\code{pool} at
\((32,224)\): four spikes, to 3.3); their feature-usage entropy is the hard model's (\(0.79\), \(0.94\); hard
\(0.73\), \(0.93\); \code{pool} \(0.59\), \(0.89\)).

\section{What the measurements show}

\textbf{The conflict is the placement, not the kernel.}  RBLapSum relaxes a gate whose output, in the code-carried
stack, is also a skip connection, and the relaxation is multiplied along it, \(1+b/2T\) per gate.  Putting the
probabilities where they were in the stream-carried stack -- on what the decoder reads -- and leaving the carry's
backward the mask removes the product exactly, in one pass; trained, the local scope keeps the unmodified
surrogate's whole gain at \(K=32\) (first order keeps half) and removes four fifths of its loss at \(K=256\).

\textbf{The carry gives the surrogate what the stream stack could not: a downstream for the entry term.}  In the
code stack an entering feature is carried, so the first-order value of admitting it is the sum of the reads it would
reach -- a sum the stream stack, which re-encodes at every block, does not have.  The mixed scope evaluates inactive candidates on that sum and active members
on the reads their run reaches: it doubles the gain at \(K=32\) and turns the loss at \(K=256\) into a gain that is
still widening at 10k, with the unmodified backward's surrogate energy (\(\sigma=0.74\) against \(0.70\)) delivered as
a sum of single-flip terms instead of a product.  The unmodified backward was not too strong; it was multiplying.

\textbf{The model uses the carry only when the surrogate leaves it alone.}  \code{pool}-trained models re-select
\(46\)--\(70\%\) of their support at every gate (hard: about half) and end with the stream stack's code, which is why
the carry was worth nothing to them; every carry-scope model has the hard model's survival and turnover, so the
support signal and the carry's persistence add instead of competing.  \textbf{Scope:} one seed, one family (8 layers, \(N=1536\), \(T=2\), no output norm), 3000-step screens and two 10k
continuations against step-matched 20k baselines from another machine of the same GPU generation; untested: \(T=1\), the
post-norm arm, the hard and persistent drivers alone \PH{}, depths beyond eight blocks.
