{\large\bfseries A surrogate for the carried code: RBLapSum on the branch, the carry left hard}\\[1pt]
{\small 8-layer TinyStories models of the K+J notes, \code{code\_residual}, abs-TopK over \(N=1536\), \(T=2\), MD optimizer, one seed.
Runs \code{be\_cr\_*} (box bergen), configs \code{configs/cr\_kj/carry/}, \code{rblapsum\_surrogate\_scope=carry\_*}.}\par}

\section{The problem}

With the code carried between blocks (\code{code\_residual}) hard Top-\(K'\) gains \(0.06\)--\(0.075\) nats at every
\(K'\) and RBLapSum (\(K\) active, pool \(K{+}J\)) gains \(-0.03\) to \(+0.015\): at a matched active count it is worth
\(0.047\) at \(K=32\), loses \(0.02\)--\(0.03\) at \(K=128,256\), no cell reaches hard Top-\((K{+}J)\), and its backward
is the unstable part of the grid (\code{docs/kj-vs-hard-topk-code-residual.tex}).  The first-order scope removes the
instability with a second backward pass and is \emph{worse} than the unmodified surrogate at every \(K=32\) cell
(\(+0.007\) to \(+0.017\) at 20k).  This note locates the conflict and removes it at no cost.

\section{Where the probabilities act}

Gate \(\ell\) reads \(u_\ell=c_\ell+v_\ell\) (carried code plus the block's encoded contribution) and outputs
\(c_{\ell+1}=M_\ell u_\ell\), \(M_\ell\) the Top-\(K\) mask.  Its output has two consumers, the carry
\(u_{\ell+1}=c_{\ell+1}+v_{\ell+1}\) (a skip path) and the read \(x_{\ell+1}=\mathrm N(D_\ell c_{\ell+1})\) (a branch):
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial u_\ell}=M_\ell\bigl(g^{\rm c}_{\ell+1}+r_{\ell+1}\bigr),\qquad
  g^{\rm c}_{\ell+1}=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial u_{\ell+1}},\quad
  r_{\ell+1}=D_\ell^{\!\top}\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(D_\ell c_{\ell+1})}
  \label{eq:hard}
\end{equation}
(at the last gate \(r\) is the unembedding's gradient).  In the stream-carried stack the gate has only the second
consumer: everything block \(\ell{+}1\) does, its own gate included, goes through \(D_\ell\).  RBLapSum relaxes the gate
as a whole, \(M_\ell\to M_\ell+S_\ell\) (support term \(S_\ell\) of \code{through\_rank\_kappa}, kernel
\(\kappa_i=e^{-|s_i-b|/T}/2T\), \(S_{ii}=|u_i|\kappa_i\approx b/2T\) at the boundary).  In the stream stack \(S_\ell\)
acts on \(r_{\ell+1}\) alone and reaches gate \(\ell{-}1\) through \(E_\ell\), the block and \(D_{\ell-1}\), which mix
the coordinates.  In the code stack it also acts on \(g^{\rm c}_{\ell+1}\), which already contains
\(S_{\ell+1}g^{\rm c}_{\ell+2}\): along a coordinate near the boundary the backward multiplies \(1+|u|\kappa\) per gate.
The carry is a residual connection in code space, trainable because its backward is the identity; a relaxation
placed on the skip path compounds.  The conflict is \emph{where} the probabilities act, not the kernel.

\section{The carry-aware surrogate}

Keep the skip path hard, relax the branch: the gate's output is split into the copy the next gate carries and the
copy the decoder reads (forward and inference unchanged), and
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial u_\ell}=M_\ell\bigl(g^{\rm c}_{\ell+1}+r_{\ell+1}\bigr)+S_\ell^{\!\top}\hat g_{\ell+1},
  \qquad
  \hat g_{\ell+1,i}=r_{\ell+1,i}+\begin{cases}0 & \text{local}\\ H_{\ell+1,i} & \text{hard}\\ \rho R_{\ell+1,i} & \text{persistent,}\end{cases}
  \label{eq:carry}
\end{equation}
with \(H_\ell=M_\ell(r_{\ell+1}+H_{\ell+1})\) the downstream read gradients a coordinate reaches along the hard carry,
\(R_\ell=r_{\ell+1}+\rho R_{\ell+1}\) every downstream read with a survival factor \(\rho\) per gate, \(H=R=0\) above
the last gate.  The support term is driven by read gradients only, so no kernel sees another gate's support term
through the carry: the products are gone, the terms of the gates above still reach gate \(\ell\) added through
\(M_\ell\), and carry and update receive one gradient (R1 of \code{docs/rblapsum-code-residual.tex}); the cost is two
per-coordinate sums along the backward, no second pass.  The drivers are the first-order loss change of one flip at
gate \(\ell\) under three models of what follows (\(c_{\ell+1,i}\) changes by \(\pm u_{\ell,i}\); block \(\ell{+}1\)
reads it, later blocks read it while the coordinate stays in the code).  \emph{Local}: the next read only, the stream
stack's logic, exactly the gradient of a model whose decoder reads a relaxed gate while the carry is hard.
\emph{Hard}: the actual downstream masks (an evicted feature's remaining run; for an inactive candidate almost always
nothing).  \emph{Persistent}: an entering coordinate survives each further gate with probability \(\rho\).
\emph{Mixed}: hard for the active members, persistent for the inactive ones; \(\rho\) is measured below.  The
first-order estimator needs the fully hard gradient at every gate, hence a second pass; here the carry is where the
gradient is taken hard, and the reads still carry the gates above through the branch, as in the stream stack.

\section{Measurements}

\textbf{Setup.}  The code-carried \(T=2\) arm of the K+J grid (8\(\times\)768, \(N=1536\), \code{residual\_out} at
every block, no output norm, batch \(96\times512\), 20k-step cosine schedule, seed 1337).  Baselines are the grid's
runs (hard Top-\(K'\) with the post-norm, RBLapSum \code{pool}, the \code{first\_order} sweep) read at matching steps;
each new scope trains the grid's config with one field changed, stopped at step 3000 with the schedules untouched;
two cells are continued to 10k.  Step 3000 is where the grid's surrogate margins are largest and already of their
final sign (\code{pool}: \(-0.061\to-0.042\) from 3k to 20k at \(K=32\), \(+0.048\to+0.034\) at \(K=256\)).

\textbf{At initialization} (Fig.~\ref{fig:mech}a,b) the surrogate's share of the gradient energy at the gates,
\(\sigma=\lVert S^\top\hat g\rVert^2/\lVert m\odot g\rVert^2\), is \(0.70\) for \code{pool}, \(0.74\) for mixed,
\(0.31\) for local and \(0.24\) for first order at \(K=32\) (\(0.30\), \(0.20\), \(0.09\), \(0.09\) at \(K=256\)): the
mixed scope injects as much support signal as the unmodified backward, as a sum instead of a product.

\textbf{Runs of the carried code} (Fig.~\ref{fig:mech}c,d; 16 validation sequences, hard forward).  The carry is
persistent for a subset of the code, not for the code: in the trained hard Top-32 model every gate evicts
\(30\)--\(54\%\) of the support it receives, an entered feature is still there one gate later with probability
\(0.51\) and two gates later \(0.34\), \(12\%\) of the final code was carried from gate 0 and \(54\%\) entered at the
last gate (hard Top-256: \(0.57\), \(0.41\), \(17\%\), \(34\%\)); an entry at gate 0 is read by \(2.7\)--\(3.1\) blocks
on average, against 8 under the persistent model and 1 under the local one.  The \code{pool} surrogate trains the
model \emph{out of} the carry -- at \((32,224)\) turnover \(63\%\) per gate at step 4000 and \(70\%\) at 20k, survival
\(0.21\to0.16\), \(77\%\) of the final code entering at the last gate: the stream stack's re-selection at every block,
in a model that has a carry -- which is why the carry was ``nearly neutral'' for it in the grid.  Every carry-scope
model keeps the hard model's code: survival \(0.52\)--\(0.61\), turnover \(30\)--\(40\%\) at step 3000 (hard:
\(0.60\)--\(0.62\), \(28\)--\(31\%\) at 4000).

\textbf{Training} (Fig.~\ref{fig:training}, Table~\ref{tab:screens}).  At \((32,224)\) the local scope ends step 3000
at \(-0.058\) below hard Top-32, where \code{pool} is at \(-0.061\) and first order at \(-0.033\); it leads \code{pool}
by \(0.02\) at step 1000 and the two coincide from 2000.  The mixed scope ends at \(-0.118\), twice the margin of the
unmodified surrogate and the margin of hard Top-128 over hard Top-32 at that step (\(-0.124\)): 32 active features at
the loss of the hard model that activates 128 (\(1.782\) against \(1.776\)).  Continued to 10k steps its margin
decays as the grid's do, \(-0.109\) at 5k and \PH{} at 10k, against hard Top-128 at \(-0.121\) and \(-0.117\) and

its code turning over \(46\%\) per gate against the hard model's \(28\%\)), the local scope closes the gap to
\(+0.009\) (\(+0.065\), \(+0.030\), \(+0.016\), \(+0.009\) at steps 500--3000, against \(+0.133\), \(+0.087\),
\(+0.057\), \(+0.048\)) and the mixed scope is below hard Top-256 from step 2000: \(-0.0045\) at 3000 (\(1.736\)
against \(1.741\)), a surrogate ahead of the hard model with the same active count, which no code-carried cell of

\section{What the measurements show}

\textbf{The conflict is the placement, not the kernel.}  RBLapSum relaxes a gate whose output, in the code-carried
stack, is also a skip connection, and the relaxation is multiplied along the skip path, \(1+b/2T\) per gate
(Fig.~\ref{fig:mech}a,b).  Putting the probabilities where they were in the stream-carried stack -- on what the decoder
reads -- and leaving the carry's backward the mask removes the product exactly, in one backward pass, with one
gradient for carry and update.  Trained, the local scope keeps the unmodified surrogate's whole gain at \(K=32\)
(first order, which also removes the product but needs a second pass, keeps half of it) and four fifths of the
unmodified surrogate's loss at \(K=256\) disappears, without a gradient spike in either cell.

\textbf{The carry gives the surrogate what the stream stack could not: a downstream for the entry term.}  In the
stream stack a candidate entering at gate \(\ell\) is re-encoded by gate \(\ell{+}1\), so the next read is all there is
to evaluate it against.  In the code stack an entering feature is carried, and the first-order value of admitting it
is the sum of the reads it would reach.  The mixed scope evaluates inactive candidates on that sum and active
members on the reads their run actually reaches; it doubles the gain at \(K=32\) and turns the loss at \(K=256\)
into a gain, with the unmodified backward's surrogate energy (\(\sigma=0.74\) against \(0.70\)) delivered as a sum of
single-flip terms instead of a product.  The unmodified backward was not too strong; it was multiplying.

\textbf{The surrogate-trained model uses the carry only when the surrogate leaves the carry alone.}  Trained hard
models carry about half of their support from gate to gate; \code{pool}-trained ones re-select \(46\)--\(70\%\) of it
at every gate and end with the stream stack's code, which is why the carry was worth nothing to them.  Every
carry-scope model has the hard model's survival and turnover: the surrogate's support signal and the carry's
persistence add instead of competing.

\textbf{Scope.}  One seed, one family (8 layers, \(N=1536\), \(T=2\), no output norm), 3000-step screens and two 10k
continuations against step-matched 20k baselines trained on another machine of the same GPU generation; the only
calibration of what a 3000-step margin becomes is the grid's own (\code{pool}: \(-0.061\to-0.042\), \(+0.048\to+0.034\))
and the continuations (\PH{}).  Not tested: \(T=1\) and the post-norm arm, the ``hard'' and ``persistent'' drivers
alone \PH{}, DDP (the chain is per process; nothing in it is rank-global), and depths beyond eight blocks, where the
24-layer stack is the case the product first collapsed.
